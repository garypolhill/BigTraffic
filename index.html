Big Traffic!

Welcome to Big Traffic, which provides access to some videos of a simple traffic simulation using maps of the road network of the central belt of Scotland. Note that these are not based on real data about journeys made in the central belt, but are intended instead to demonstrate some of the capabilities of agent-based modelling for visualisation -- especially in an immersive suite.

You can choose between four versions of the video:

  1. Smaller video at 4K resolution
  2. Smaller video at 8K resolution
